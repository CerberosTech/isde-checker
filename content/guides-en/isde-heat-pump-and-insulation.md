Planning a heat pump and insulation? That can make a real difference to your ISDE subsidy. These are RVO's rules.

## The rule: the insulation amount doubles

Your subsidy for **insulation** doubles if you carry out more than one insulation measure, or if you combine an insulation measure with a **heat pump**, solar water heater or a connection to a heat network [1][2]. The condition: you carry out the next measure **within 24 months** of the previous one [1][2].

What does not double:

- the amount for the **heat pump** itself: the doubling applies to the insulation amount [1][3];
- the **biobased bonus** for eco-friendly insulation material [1];
- insulation combined with **ventilation** only [1].

## RVO's worked example

RVO gives this example [1]: 36 m² of biobased roof insulation in 2025 comes to 36 × €16.25 = **€585**, plus a biobased bonus. Add a heat pump and only the €585 is doubled. Including the bonus, RVO arrives at a total of **€1,350** for the roof insulation, plus the amount for the heat pump.

## Amounts per insulation measure (carried out in 2025 or later)

According to RVO, before doubling [1]:

- **Cavity wall insulation:** €5.25 per m² (minimum / maximum 10 / 170 m²)
- **Facade insulation:** €20.25 per m² (minimum / maximum 10 / 170 m²)
- **Roof insulation:** €16.25 per m² (minimum / maximum 20 / 200 m²)
- **Floor insulation:** €5.50 per m² (minimum / maximum 20 / 130 m²)
- **Crawl space (ground) insulation:** €3 per m² (minimum / maximum 20 / 130 m²)
- **Attic or loft floor insulation:** €4 per m² (minimum / maximum 20 / 130 m²)
- **HR++ glazing (without new frames):** €25 per m² (minimum / maximum 3 / 45 m² (all glazing combined))
- **Triple or vacuum glazing in new frames:** €111 per m² (minimum / maximum 3 / 45 m² (all glazing combined))

For crawl space and floor insulation, and for roof and attic floor insulation, the 20 m² minimum applies to both combined [1].

## Order and timing

- The 24 months run from when the previous measure was carried out [3].
- You apply for each measure within 24 months of it being carried out [1][2].
- Live in Groningen province or in Aa en Hunze, Noordenveld or Tynaarlo? Then you cannot get ISDE subsidy for insulation (a different scheme applies there), but you may still get it for a heat pump [1].
