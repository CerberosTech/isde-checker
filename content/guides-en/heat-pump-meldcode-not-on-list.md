Can't find your heat pump on RVO's notification code list? That does not necessarily mean you won't get a subsidy.

## First: search again carefully

- Search on the **exact model number** from the quote or invoice, not just the brand name. For an outdoor unit paired with an indoor unit, the list sometimes shows the combination.
- Search [our heat pump list](https://cerberostech.github.io/isde-checker/en/heat-pumps/) by brand, type or meldcode, or browse by brand via [all brands](https://cerberostech.github.io/isde-checker/en/brands/).
- Ask your installer: RVO advises having the meldcode put on the quote and the invoice [2].

More tips in [How to look up a heat pump's meldcode](heat-pump-meldcode-lookup).

## Not on the list? You can still apply

According to RVO, you can apply without a meldcode too. You then include the heat pump's **product description and technical documentation** [1][3]. You also provide the usual details: model number and brand, invoice and proof of payment, installation date and the installation company's details [1].

**Note:** RVO then assesses for itself whether the heat pump meets the requirements. If it does not, your application is rejected and you get no subsidy [1][3]. So ideally, check before you buy that the model is on the list [1].

## Why isn't a model on the list?

Possible reasons, based on RVO's requirements [4]:

- **Air-to-air heat pump:** not eligible [4][5].
- **Energy label below A++:** at least A++ has been required since 1 January 2024 [4].
- **Not registered in EPREL:** this is mandatory up to and including 70 kW thermal capacity [4].
- **70 kW or more:** new heat pumps of 70 kW or more are no longer added to the list; a product description is required instead [4].
- **Split system with a high-GWP refrigerant:** from 2026, split air-to-water heat pumps with a refrigerant charge under 3 kg and a GWP above 750 are no longer eligible [1].
- **New model:** the list is updated monthly [4]. A manufacturer can submit a product for the list [4].

How the rest of the application works: [How to apply for the ISDE subsidy: step by step](isde-application-step-by-step).
