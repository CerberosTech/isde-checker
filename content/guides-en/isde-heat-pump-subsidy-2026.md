# ISDE heat pump subsidy 2026: how much do you get?

With the ISDE (Investment Subsidy for Sustainable Energy and Energy Saving, Investeringssubsidie duurzame energie en energiebesparing), homeowners get money back on a (hybrid) heat pump. The amount depends on the type of heat pump you have installed. Below is what RVO says about it, as checked on 17 October 2026.

## How much subsidy do you get?

- You **always get at least €500** for a (hybrid) heat pump [1].
- The exact amount varies by model. RVO does not give a fixed amount per type: you will find an indicative amount for each heat pump on the **heat pump notification code list** (meldcodelijst warmtepompen) [1][3]. See also our guide [How to look up a heat pump's meldcode](heat-pump-meldcode-lookup).
- Look up your model or meldcode in [our heat pump list](https://cerberostech.github.io/isde-checker/en/heat-pumps/), or browse [all brands on the ISDE list](https://cerberostech.github.io/isde-checker/en/brands/).
- Doing several measures? Use RVO's ISDE calculation tool for an estimate of the total [1][2].

### Exceptions in 2026

- **Bought in 2024, installed in 2025 or 2026?** A transitional arrangement applies: with proof of purchase in 2024 (for example an order confirmation or a digitally signed quote), you qualify for the higher 2024 subsidy amount [1].
- **Second or subsequent air-to-water heat pump in 2026?** You get a lower amount, because the calculation changed in 2026. This applies even if you already received a subsidy for the first one. If you installed that extra heat pump in 2024 or 2025 and apply in 2026, the old amounts apply [1].
- **Split air-to-water heat pumps** with a refrigerant charge under 3 kilograms and a refrigerant with a GWP above 750 are no longer eligible from 2026, because of EU rules [1].

### Combining with insulation

Also insulating within 24 months? Then the subsidy for the insulation measure doubles. The 24 months run from when the previous measure was carried out [1][2].

## Conditions

According to RVO [1][2][3]:

- You are an **owner-occupier**: you own the home and live in it yourself.
- The heat pump is **new** and used for space heating or hot water (a heat pump tumble dryer does not count).
- A **building installation company** carries out the entire installation; you may not install it yourself.
- The home was **built before 1 January 2019**, or the environmental permit was applied for before 1 July 2018.
- Air-to-air heat pumps are excluded; since 1 January 2024 a heat pump must have at least an A++ energy label.

## Application deadline

You have the heat pump installed first and then apply for the subsidy, **no later than 24 months after installation** [1][2]. RVO aims to process an application within 8 weeks [2]. How it works in practice: [How to apply for the ISDE subsidy: step by step](isde-application-step-by-step).

## Note: changes in 2027

RVO says the ISDE will change on a number of points from 1 January 2027. Check the current conditions on rvo.nl if your installation falls around the turn of the year [2].
