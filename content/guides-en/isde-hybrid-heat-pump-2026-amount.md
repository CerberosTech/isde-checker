A hybrid heat pump works alongside your gas boiler. For the ISDE it counts as an air-to-water heat pump: RVO covers the (hybrid) heat pump on the same conditions page [1]. Here is how the amount is calculated in 2026.

## The 2026 formula

For your **first** air-to-water heat pump installed in 2026, RVO says you get [2]:

- a **base amount of €1,025** (it was €1,250 in 2025);
- **€225 per kW** of capacity, from the first kW (in 2025 this only counted from the second kW);
- an **energy label bonus of €200** for an A+++ label.

You always get at least **€500** for a (hybrid) heat pump [1].

## Worked example

RVO gives this example itself: a first air-to-water heat pump with an A+++ label and a capacity of 4 kW comes to **€1,025 + 4 × €225 + €200 = €2,125** [2].

The exact capacity and amount for your model are on RVO's notification code list [1][3]. You can also look up your model in [our heat pump list](https://cerberostech.github.io/isde-checker/en/heat-pumps/), for example the [Intergas Xtend 5](https://cerberostech.github.io/isde-checker/en/heat-pumps/intergas-xtend-5/) or the [Remeha Elga Ace 4 kW](https://cerberostech.github.io/isde-checker/en/heat-pumps/remeha-elga-ace-4-kw/). Always check the amount in the notification code list or RVO's ISDE calculation tool [4].

## Exceptions

- **Second or subsequent air-to-water heat pump in 2026:** no base amount and no label bonus, only €225 per kW. So a second 4 kW heat pump gets 4 × €225 = €900 [2].
- **Bought in 2024, installed in 2025 or 2026:** with proof of purchase or order before 1 January 2025, you get the 2024 amounts [1][5].
- **Split systems:** from 2026, split air-to-water heat pumps with a refrigerant charge under 3 kg and a GWP above 750 are no longer eligible [2].

## Conditions in brief

The heat pump is new, a building installation company installs it, the home was built before 1 January 2019 (or the permit was applied for before 1 July 2018), and you apply within 24 months of installation [1].
