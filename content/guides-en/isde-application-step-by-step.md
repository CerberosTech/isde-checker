# How to apply for the ISDE subsidy: step by step

RVO (the Netherlands Enterprise Agency) sets out the ISDE application for homeowners in five steps [1]. Here they are, focused on a heat pump.

## Step 1: Check the conditions

You must own the home and live in it yourself, the heat pump must be new and installed by a building installation company, and the home must have been built before 1 January 2019 (or the environmental permit applied for before 1 July 2018) [1][2]. For amounts and exceptions, see [ISDE heat pump subsidy 2026](isde-heat-pump-subsidy-2026).

## Step 2: Find the notification code (meldcode)

Before you buy, check that the heat pump is on RVO's notification code list (meldcodelijst) [1]. See [How to look up a heat pump's meldcode](heat-pump-meldcode-lookup). You can also search for your meldcode directly in [our heat pump list](https://cerberostech.github.io/isde-checker/en/heat-pumps/).

## Step 3: Buy the heat pump

- Ask the installer to put the meldcode on both the quote and the invoice.
- Keep your proof of purchase (for example a signed quote or an order confirmation by email) and your proof of payment, such as a bank statement [1].

## Step 4: Have the heat pump installed

Install first, then apply. You are not allowed to install it yourself [1]. Make sure the eligible components are itemised separately on the invoice [1].

## Step 5: Apply for the subsidy

What you need [1][2]:

- **The DigiD app or DigiD with SMS verification.** Sort this out in good time: it can take a few working days.
- The **meldcode**, or if there is none, the product description and technical documentation.
- A description with the model number and brand or manufacturer.
- **Invoice and proof of payment** showing the purchase date, amount and payment date(s).
- The **installation date**.
- Your address and the installation company's details (address, contact person, phone number).

Submit your application **no later than 24 months after installation**. RVO recommends using a computer or tablet; once logged in, the form is under 'Nieuwe aanvraag' (New application) [1].

## After you apply

- You get an acknowledgement by email with a reference number [1].
- RVO aims to decide **within 8 weeks**. If it cannot, you will receive a letter about an extension after about 7 weeks [1].
- Changes (such as your bank account number) or withdrawing your application are done via 'Mijn aanvragen' (My applications) [1].
- You can authorise someone to apply on your behalf using RVO's authorisation form; you keep that form yourself [1].
