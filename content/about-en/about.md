## Who makes this site?

ISDE Checker is a small, independent project by CerberosTech. The site is not affiliated with RVO, the Dutch government, manufacturers or installers. There is no editorial team behind it: the data comes from public sources, and the site is rebuilt automatically.

## Sources

- The **meldcodelijst warmtepompen** (the list of heat pump meldcodes, or notification codes) from RVO (Rijksdienst voor Ondernemend Nederland, the Netherlands Enterprise Agency). The meldcodes, brands, types and subsidy amounts come from this list.
- The ISDE conditions and calculation rules on RVO.nl.
- Each guide lists the sources it is based on.

## How we check the data

- Meldcodes and amounts are taken from the most recent RVO list that we have fetched. We never make up or estimate amounts.
- Guides show an "Updated on" date for the last change to their content.
- Only RVO decides on your application. Always check the meldcode and the amount in the current list on RVO.nl before you buy or apply. Note that the RVO application form is in Dutch.

## Affiliate links

Some pages contain affiliate links, for example to bol.com, a Dutch web shop. If you buy something through one of these links, we may receive a small commission. You pay nothing extra, and it has no influence on the meldcodes, amounts or explanations.
